# A five-minute customer demo

## Positioning
“Relay Voice gives your team a consistent way to follow up: an agent with your business context, a defined conversation, and a clear next step for a human.”

Say at the outset: “Today’s workspace uses fictional contacts and simulated conversations. We are demonstrating the workflow, not placing telephone calls.”

## Suggested story
**0:00 — See the operation.** Show the overview. Explain that the displayed results are seed examples plus this session’s simulations. Avoid implying these are customer conversion numbers.

**0:45 — Make the agent theirs.** Open Aria’s configuration. Change the opening or qualification question to match a fictional customer’s business. Explain that industry, use case and context can be customized. The natural-language system prompt is stored for the future model runtime; the demo’s conversation behavior is scripted.

**1:30 — Have a conversation.** In Call studio, select Aria and Jamie Morgan. Start. Click Happy to talk → Share a need → Confirm next step. Show the qualified outcome and follow-up task. Type a question with a question mark to demonstrate how the simulator uses business context. Keep this distinct from claiming general-purpose AI reasoning.

**2:30 — Show judgment and control.** Start another simulation and ask for a human. It records a handoff request without pretending to connect an actual person. Show the opt-out option and explain server-side suppression. Use a different contact if demonstrating opt-out, because that contact becomes ineligible for future demos until explicitly edited.

**3:15 — Repeat the workflow.** Run New lead discovery. It skips contacts without consent and creates local tasks. Mark a task done. Calls are instant scripted runs; not a real scheduled campaign.

**4:00 — Close the loop.** Search the history, open the transcript and configuration snapshot, export the transcript. Show analytics and point out the metric definitions. If an on-device English voice is available, play the transcript; otherwise use the visible transcript. Browser audio is optional and not a prerequisite for the pitch.

**4:45 — Discuss the pilot.** Choose one vertical, one measurable operational outcome, one approved provider, and a limited consenting test audience. Explain the next steps: existing voice-worker extraction, provider sandbox validation, and controlled live pilot.

## Commercial discovery questions
Which calls are most repetitive? What makes a qualified lead or resolved request? When must a human intervene? Where should the next step appear? What consent evidence exists? Which languages and calling hours matter? What current cost or missed-opportunity baseline will the pilot compare against?

## Avoid unsupported claims
Do not promise production accuracy, cost savings, latency, compliance, real-time speech quality, CRM integration or automatic scheduling from this demo. Treat pricing and the Relay Voice brand as provisional until positioning and clearance are complete.
