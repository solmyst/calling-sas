Relay Voice: configurable AI voice conversations for sales, customer help, customer care and appointment intake.

Built with Gnani Prisma STT, Bifrost and Gnani Timbre TTS. This demo shows browser voice, automatic barge-in with Silero VAD, and conversation memory that keeps the latest correction.

Real app. Fictional demo data. Appointment requests stay requests until a human confirms availability.
